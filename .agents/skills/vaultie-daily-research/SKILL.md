# Vaultie Daily Research

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `.agent/AGENTS.md`, `90_Meta/hot.md`, `90_Meta/_index.md` as applicable. Read `.agent/watchlist.yaml` if it exists.
2. Search existing Vaultie notes and recent clips before searching the web.
3. Prioritize official documentation, releases, changelogs and security advisories. Compare publication date with event date.
4. Limit the report to a small high-signal set (default maximum 5 candidates). Exclude duplicates, weakly sourced claims, marketing-only posts and items that do not affect the owner's learning/projects.
5. For every candidate capture URL, source date/access date, relevant version, claim, evidence, why it matters, limitations, confidence and follow-up.
6. Write dated reports and candidates only to `00_Inbox/_agent_bus/reports/` and `00_Inbox/_clips/`. Do not publish to canonical notes.
7. Finish with new items, rejected items and reason, unresolved uncertainties, and suggested next action.

Do not alter the watchlist automatically; propose edits separately. Do not send outside notifications or commit changes.
